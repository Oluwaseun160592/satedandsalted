Our Story

Why We Are Called
SATED & SALTED

We believe that a truly unforgettable meal does two essential things: it leaves you completely Sated (deeply satisfied and nourished), and is seasoned and Salted to culinary perfection.

Born out of a deep love for bold Nigerian street food and hearty comfort bowls, Sated & Salted bridges the gap between home-cooked warmth and restaurant precision. Whether you are craving the deep, smoky aromatics of party Jollof spaghetti, the crispy golden crunch of egg-battered Yamarita, or the spicy kick of tender Gizdodo, we prepare every bowl as if cooking for family.
